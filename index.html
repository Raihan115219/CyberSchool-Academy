About our academy

Empowering the Next Generation of Cybersecurity Professionals

Founded in 2015, CyberSchool has trained over 10,000 cybersecurity professionals who now work at leading global organizations.

Our Mission

To democratize cybersecurity education and create a world where skilled security professionals can defend digital infrastructure against evolving threats. We bridge the gap between theory and industry practice through hands-on training and mentorship.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted cybersecurity education platform, producing competent professionals who make the internet safer. We envision a future where every organization has access to top-tier security talent trained at CyberSchool.
